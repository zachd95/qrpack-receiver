How this works

On the computer run py qrpack.py encode <file or folder>. It opens a page that cycles through QR codes. Hold this camera steady on it until every square above turns green.

Missed a few? They come round again on the next loop. To save time, type the missing numbers into the player's Only show frames box.

Printed or photographed codes work too: use Add photos.
